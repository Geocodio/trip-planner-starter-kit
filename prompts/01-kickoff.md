# 01 Kickoff

Use this first, in a fresh Claude Code session opened in this repo. It makes Claude interview you before it plans anything.

```
I want to plan a trip with you and end up with a private offline app for my family. Read CLAUDE.md, trip/family.md and trip/trip.json (the example) first.

Before you plan anything, interview me with multiple-choice questions, a few at a time. Cover:
- who is coming (ages, interests, energy, anyone with mobility or food needs)
- where, when, and why (holiday, conference, wedding, visiting family)
- fixed points: flights, event dates, hotels already booked
- how we travel on the ground (rental car, trains, walking)
- pace: how many big things per day, how light the arrival days should be
- budget and anything we refuse to do

When you have enough, write my answers into trip/family.md, replace the example in trip/trip.json with a skeleton for my trip (days, fixed points, empty items), and show me what you changed.
```
