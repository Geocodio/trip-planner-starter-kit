# 02 Research activities

Use after the kickoff, when you have fixed points and a family profile. Output is a long list of ideas, not a plan.

```
Using trip/family.md, research things to do in <region>. Give me 40 ideas grouped by area. For each idea:
- What it actually is: one plain sentence on what we physically do there, how long it takes, and how much walking.
- The story: why it exists or why people care, in two sentences.
- Why it fits us: tie it to a named person and their interests, or say honestly that it is a stretch.
- Whether it needs a booking or a timed ticket.

Be honest about weak ideas. Do not guess opening times or prices; say "check" when you are not sure. Put the list in ideas.md (not in trip.json yet).
```
