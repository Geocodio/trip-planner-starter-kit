# 04 Book and check list

Use as soon as the destination and dates are fixed, and again after every booking. Entry rules change, so this is where mistakes cost real money.

```
Build the book-and-check list for this trip and store it in trip.json under "checklist". Cover everything beyond flights and hotels:
- visas, electronic travel authorizations and entry declarations (with the deadline for each, and the official site)
- driving permits for the rental car
- travel insurance that covers the specific activities we plan
- health or waiver forms for activities
- timed tickets, with the booking window
- rental pickup and return times
- passport validity rules

For each item write: what it is, when to do it (a deadline), and a note on the trap people fall into. Say where you are not sure of a current rule and name the official page to check. Then tell me which items to do first.
```
