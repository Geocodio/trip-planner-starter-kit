# 03 Family vote

Use after you have the ideas list. Everyone votes on their phone or on paper, and the winners go into the plan.

```
Turn ideas.md into a family vote. Build vote.html: one card per idea, with the "what it actually is" line first, then the story, then a Yes / Maybe / No choice per person from trip/family.md. Votes are stored in localStorage and shown as a small summary at the top. One static file, no server, big tap targets, readable on a phone.

When I tell you the results, update trip.json: keep the Yes and Maybe ideas as candidate items and drop the rest.
```
